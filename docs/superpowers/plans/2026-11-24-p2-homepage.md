# P2: Homepage around customer needs Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Rewrite Home as hero → 3-step journey → practical Q&A → (existing roadmap) → close, using real app screenshots.

**Architecture:** One FAQ list, `src/faq.ts`, feeds both `/faq` and Home, so they can't drift apart. Hero keeps its copy and backdrop. The interactive demo is deleted and one framed screenshot replaces it. Screens live in `src/assets/screens/` and go through `astro:assets` `<Image>`.

**Tech Stack:** Astro 7, plain CSS. **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-website-priorities.md` (P2), plus the copy approved in chat on 2026-11-24.

## Global Constraints

- Step names: "Snap & Diagnose", "Match & Compare", "Get Fixed & Review". At most 2 sentences each, copy exactly as approved.
- AI disclaimer under step 1: "AI-generated from your photos and answers. Not a substitute for a hands-on inspection."
- Screens are captioned "Screens from the RevX app · demo accounts".
- No claims beyond the spec's product truths. "Free forever" is gone everywhere.
- P3/P4/P6 sections are out of scope; `#vision` stays until P6.

## Review Focus

1. Hero at 375px: the screenshot must not push the CTAs below the first viewport. It goes under the copy and is capped in height.
2. Image weight: `swipe.png` is 696KB. It must ship as responsive webp, not the raw PNG.
3. `/faq` keeps FAQPage JSON-LD built from the same list, and Home emits none.
4. Old `#how` redirect still works (Home script untouched).
5. Alt text describes each screen's content, not "screenshot".
